# Chatglide privacy policy

_Last updated: September 29, 2026_

Chatglide is a browser extension that shows YouTube live chat as an overlay on top of the video.

## What Chatglide does not do

- It does not collect, store, sell or share any personal data.
- It does not send anything to any server. The extension has no backend and makes no network requests of its own.
- It does not use analytics, tracking, cookies or advertising.
- It does not read your YouTube account, watch history, or anything outside the video page's chat.

## What stays on your device

- **Your settings** (overlay position, size, colors, language and so on) are saved with the browser's extension storage (`chrome.storage.local`) on your own device. Uninstalling the extension removes them.
- **Chat messages** that YouTube already shows on the page are read from the page and displayed in the overlay while you watch. They are kept only in memory and discarded when you leave the video.

## Permissions

- **Storage**: to remember your settings.
- **Access to youtube.com**: to read the chat YouTube displays and show it over the video player. The extension runs only on youtube.com.

## Contact

Questions or concerns: open an issue in the project's GitHub repository.
